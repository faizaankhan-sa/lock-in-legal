# Lock In — Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to tell us something? Email **hello.lockinapp@gmail.com**. We usually reply within two working days, and we look at reports of abuse within 24 hours.

## Lock In Pro (subscription)

- **What it is:** everything is free for your first 7 days, with nothing to sign up for. After that, starting focus sessions needs Lock In Pro, monthly or yearly. Reviewing your friends' photos, your History and your account stay free.
- **Cancel:** on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lock In Pro, or in the app, Account → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Lock In Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Refunds:** Apple handles them at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **New phone or reinstalled the app:** tap **Restore purchases** (Account tab, or the Lock In Pro screen) while signed in to the same Apple ID.

## Proof checks

- **Turn on notifications** for Lock In, including **Time Sensitive** (Settings → Notifications → Lock In). Without them, check reminders can't reach you during a session.
- Lock In needs the **camera** for proof photos. It never reads your photo library.

## Friends, reviews and safety

- **Report a photo:** in Reviews, tap the flag in the corner of the photo. It's declined straight away and hidden from you.
- **Report a comment:** in History, press and hold the comment.
- **Block someone:** in Friends, tap ⋯ next to their name. You stop being friends and partners.
- Photos nobody reviews are approved automatically after 12 hours.

## Your account and data

- **Delete your account:** Account → Delete account. Everything on our servers is deleted. The History on your phone stays.
- Read how we handle your information in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md), and the rules in the [Terms of Use](terms.md).
- Lock In is for people aged 18 and over.
