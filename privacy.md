# Lock In — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026

Lock In is a focus timer that asks you for a photo at random moments during a session to prove you're working. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It is written to meet South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the App Store and Google Play rules.

**Who is responsible:** Faizaan Khan, the developer of Lock In, based in South Africa ("we", "us"). Contact: faizaankhan.us@gmail.com. This is also how to reach our Information Officer.

## The short version

- **Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.** Your tasks, sessions, photos, scores and streak are stored only on your device.
- **Tasks are private by default.** A private task's title, sessions, photos and points never leave your phone, even if you have an account.
- **Only what you choose to share is uploaded:** for a task you share with friends, its title and the proof photos from its sessions go to the friends you picked, so they can approve or decline them.
- **Shared photos are deleted from our servers 30 days after they're reviewed.** Your phone keeps its own copy.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your information.**
- **You can delete your account in the app at any time**, and everything on our servers goes with it.

## 1. Using Lock In without an account

You don't need an account. In that case the app stores everything on your device only:

- your tasks and settings,
- your session history, scores and streak,
- the proof photos you take (camera only; the app never reads your photo library).

The app uses your phone's notifications to schedule check reminders. These are created on your phone; no server is involved. None of this information is sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app.

## 2. If you create an account

An account lets you add friends and share tasks with them. You sign in with **Sign in with Apple**. We then store:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A sign-in identifier from Apple, and the email address Apple gives us (this may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") | To sign you in and keep your account secure |
| Your username, and a display name if you add one | So friends can find and recognise you |
| Your friend list, friend requests, and people you've blocked | To run the friends feature and keep blocked people away from you |
| A push notification token for each signed-in device, and whether it's iPhone or Android | To send you notifications about reviews (for example, "your friend approved your proof") |

## 3. Shared tasks and proof photos

You can share a task with one or more friends ("partners"). Only then, and only for that task, we store:

- the task's title, and which friends are its partners,
- each proof photo taken during that task's sessions, with the time the session started and the time the photo was taken,
- each partner's verdict (approved or declined) and when it was given.

Only that task's partners can see its photos; your other friends cannot. Partners don't see each other. If nobody reviews a photo within 12 hours it is approved automatically. If you make a task private again, nothing more from it is uploaded.

Before uploading, think about what's in the frame. Avoid photos of other people who haven't agreed to it, and of anything private.

## 4. Reports

If someone reports a photo they were sent, we store the report: who made it, whose photo it was, which photo, the reason and when. We use reports only to keep Lock In safe, for example to remove content or accounts that break our [Terms of Use](terms.md). A reported photo is kept until we've reviewed the report, even if that's longer than 30 days.

## 5. How long we keep things

- **Proof photos on our servers:** deleted 30 days after they're reviewed (or auto-approved). Photos under an open report are kept until the report is resolved.
- **Everything else linked to your account:** kept while you have the account.
- **When you delete your account** (Account → Delete account, in the app): your profile, friends, blocks, shared tasks, photos, verdicts and push tokens are permanently deleted from our servers straight away. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept without anyone's name or photo, so we keep a record that a report happened.
- **On your phone:** stays until you delete it or the app. Deleting your account doesn't erase your phone's own history.

## 6. Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers who process information only to run the app for us:

- **Supabase** stores account and shared-task data and photos. Its servers are in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden).
- **Expo** delivers push notifications. Push tokens and notification text pass through Expo's service.
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service) and **Google** (Firebase Cloud Messaging, on Android) deliver sign-in and notifications.

Because these providers are outside South Africa, your information is transferred abroad. We only use providers bound by data-protection laws and agreements that give a level of protection comparable to POPIA, such as the EU's GDPR.

We don't sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or anyone else. We would disclose information only if the law required it.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by database rules, so each person can only reach what they're allowed to see. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

## 8. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

- ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy,
- ask us to correct or delete it (you can delete your account yourself, in the app),
- object to how we process it, or withdraw your consent. Doing so means deleting your account, since the social features can't run without it,
- complain to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**: <https://inforegulator.org.za>.

To use any of these rights, email faizaankhan.us@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

## 9. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Lock In's account features. If you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account. Without an account, nothing leaves the phone. If you believe a child has an account without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, for example something new would leave your phone, we'll tell you in the app first.
